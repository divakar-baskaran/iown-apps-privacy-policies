Privacy Policy

iOwn QR & Barcode Scanner · Effective October 2026

This policy describes how iOwn QR & Barcode Scanner (“the App”) handles information on your device. Core scanning and code generation run on your device. The App does not require an account.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the iOwn QR & Barcode Scanner application. It explains what information is processed when you use features such as camera scanning, gallery import, history, sharing, and downloads.

2. Information we collect or process

Depending on how you use the App, the following may be processed:

  • QR / barcode contents you scan or create — processed on-device and, if history is saved, stored locally on your device (including the full payload text).
  • Camera frames — used in real time to detect codes in the viewfinder. The App does not include a feature that uploads camera frames to our servers.
  • Photos you select — used on-device to decode a code from an image or to embed a logo in a custom QR design.
  • App preferences — for example theme mode, stored locally.

The App does not provide user registration, profiles, or cloud sync of your scan history.

3. Camera and QR / barcode scanning

Camera permission is requested so the App can detect QR codes and barcodes in real time. Scanning is performed on your device using the camera preview. Detected content is shown to you in the App and may be saved to local history.

We do not operate a backend that receives your live camera stream. If you open a URL or other actionable content decoded from a code, that action uses your device’s system browser or related apps (see Sharing, exports & links).

4. Photos, gallery, and image editing

With your permission, the App may access photos you choose in order to:

  • Decode a QR code or barcode from a gallery image
  • Import a logo or image for custom QR designs
  • Save or export generated QR / barcode images you create

Selected images are processed on your device for these features. The App does not include a feature that uploads your gallery photos to our servers.

5. Locally stored data and history

Scan and generation history (including titles, full content payloads, format/type, timestamps, and optional styling metadata) is stored locally on your device using the platform’s preferences storage. Theme preference is also stored locally.

Local history storage is intended to keep your records on the device. It is not described here as encrypted application-level storage; protect your device with your usual screen lock and OS security features.

You can delete individual history items or clear all history from within the App (Settings / History). Uninstalling the App typically removes app-local data according to your operating system.

6. Sharing, exporting, and external links

When you choose to:

  • Share text, images, or contact cards — the App uses the system share sheet; the destination app is chosen by you.
  • Download / save generated images — files may be written to Downloads, Pictures, app storage, or documents folders depending on platform and permissions.
  • Open a URL, phone number, email, SMS, or map location — the App hands off to the appropriate system app or browser.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party apps or websites you open after leaving the App.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Local history and preferences remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App (subject to OS behaviour).

If you need help with a privacy request, use the contact details below.

8. Security

We aim to handle App features responsibly and rely on platform security such as OS sandboxing. No method of electronic storage is completely secure. Protect access to your device, and clear local history if you no longer want scanned or generated content kept on the device.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating accounts — the App has no account system.

If you have a privacy concern related to a child’s use of the App, contact us using the details below so we can review the request.

10. Contact information

For privacy questions about iOwn QR & Barcode Scanner, contact:

Please include the app name and a clear description of your request.
